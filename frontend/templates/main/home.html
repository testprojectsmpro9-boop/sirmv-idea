About the Challenge

What Is This Challenge?

The Bharat Ratna Sir M. Visvesvaraya Innovator Challenge is a student innovation platform created to encourage young minds to identify real-world problems and present creative, practical, and impactful solutions.

Inspired by the vision, discipline, and engineering excellence of Sir M. Visvesvaraya, this challenge gives students an opportunity to showcase their ideas, improve their problem-solving skills, and receive recognition for their innovative thinking.

Students from different schools can submit their best ideas, projects, models, or concepts. The most promising ideas will be reviewed by experts and awarded based on creativity, feasibility, impact, and presentation.

Three categories. Infinite possibilities.

Whether you are just starting out or in advanced studies, there is a place for your ideas.

School

For schools encouraging students to submit creative and practical solutions for real-life problems.

Individual

For students who want to submit their own original idea or innovation independently.
Including school dropouts aged 6-18 years

Group

For teams of up to 2 students working together on one original idea or innovation.

For Schools

Schools Can Conduct Internal Innovation Competitions

Top 2 Innovations

Schools may organize idea competitions within their campus and submit their top 2 selected innovations to the SIR MV Young Innovators Challenge. Each innovation should be submitted separately through the submission form, and a unique Idea ID will be generated for every submission.
