Our Story

Who We Are

The Bharat Ratna Sir M. Visvesvaraya Innovator Challenge is a student-driven innovation platform designed to encourage and recognise young minds for their creative problem-solving abilities. This school-based idea competition encourages students to identify and address local, social, environmental, and everyday life challenges through innovative ideas.

The program is designed for students from Classes 1 to 10, with a special track for advanced ideas from Classes 11 and 12 science students. Named after Bharat Ratna Sir M. Visvesvaraya, the renowned engineer and visionary, the challenge promotes scientific thinking, creativity, and practical innovation.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Our mission is to foster a culture of innovation and problem-solving among school children by providing a structured pathway from idea submission to prototype development and recognition.

  • Encourage students to identify and solve real-world problems
  • Provide a platform for students to express creativity and innovation
  • Offer mentorship and prototype development opportunities
  • Recognise and reward students for creativity, feasibility, and social impact

Our Vision

The Bharat Ratna Sir M. Visvesvaraya Innovator Challenge envisions a future where young minds are equipped with the skills and confidence to tackle challenges, innovate solutions, and contribute positively to society.

  • Empower students to think scientifically and critically
  • Develop a pipeline of youth-led solutions for local and global problems
  • Identify and nurture the next generation of innovators

What We Do

How We Work

The Bharat Ratna Sir M. Visvesvaraya Innovator Challenge provides a comprehensive, step-by-step framework for students to develop and showcase their innovative ideas.

  • Idea Competitions: Students submit ideas that solve local, community, or environmental problems
  • Mentorship: Expert mentors guide students in refining ideas into viable prototypes
  • Exhibitions: Top ideas are showcased at school, district, and higher levels
  • Awards and Recognition: Winners and finalists receive certificates, awards, prototype support, and media exposure
